Get to know me!

Hello, my name is Kyi Phyu Shyun Latt (Kate). I am a Burmese native from Myanmar and a graduate in Information and Communication Technology (ICT) with a strong interest in analysis across data, business processes, systems, users, and products.

My journey in technology began in 2019 with programming fundamentals such as HTML and Java, followed by experience in graphic design. While these areas helped me understand both technical and creative aspects of IT, they led me to discover what truly motivates me: analyzing how systems work, how users interact with products, and how data reflects real business problems. This realization shaped my growing focus on analytical roles rather than purely technical development.

I actively explore different forms of analysis by working with SQL (MySQL), Excel, Power BI, Tableau, Python, and Pandas. Through hands-on projects, I have gained experience in data cleaning, exploration, visualization, and interpretation. I enjoy breaking down complex information, identifying patterns, and transforming data into insights that support better decisions and process improvements.

My academic background at Rangsit University in Bangkok has strengthened my ability to think analytically across multiple domains. In addition to data tools, I have experience with Python, Java, HTML, and CSS, allowing me to understand systems from both a technical and user-facing perspective. This helps me analyze problems holistically, considering not only numbers, but also process flows, system behavior, user needs, and product impact.

I am highly motivated by continuous learning and practical experience. I enjoy exploring new tools, frameworks, and analytical approaches, and I am eager to grow through real-world challenges. My goal is to develop into a well-rounded analyst who brings clarity, structure, and value to organizations through thoughtful analysis.

Proud of my Burmese heritage, I approach my work with resilience, curiosity, and discipline. I believe effective analysis starts with asking the right questions and I am committed to continuously learning how to ask, explore, and answer them.
